# Get in touch

Tell us a little about your business and we will send you secure upload instructions by reply.

Getting in touch does not commit you to anything. Sending us statements does not commit you to anything either. The audit is free, the analysis is yours to keep, and you are under no obligation to work with us afterwards.

---

## Before you send anything

**Do not attach statements to this form.** Card processing statements are commercially sensitive and a web form is not the right way to move them. Send us the form below, and we will reply with a secure upload link.

**What we will eventually need:** one to three months of statements covering every merchant ID you hold, in whatever format your provider issues — PDF or CSV is fine.

**How we treat them:** as confidential. We do not share them, we do not pass them to any acquirer or payment provider, and we do not use them as examples. We delete them once the audit is complete unless you ask us to hold them, and we delete them on request at any point.
