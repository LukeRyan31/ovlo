Get in touch

Tell us a little about your business and we will send you secure upload instructions by reply.

Getting in touch does not commit you to anything. Sending us statements does not commit you to anything either. The audit is free, the analysis is yours to keep, and you are under no obligation to work with us afterwards.

Email us and we will take it from there.

hello@ovlo.ie

No form, no tracking, and nothing stored on your device. Your enquiry goes straight to us.

Please do not attach statements to your first email. Card processing statements are commercially sensitive and ordinary email is not the right way to move them. Get in touch first and we will reply with a secure upload link.

Useful to include

  • Your company and your role.
  • Roughly how many sites you operate, or your approximate annual card volume.
  • Who you process with today, if you know.
  • Anything about your card costs that has prompted you to look at this.

What happens next

  1. 1We reply within one working day with a secure upload link.
  2. 2You send one to three months of statements.
  3. 3Five working days later you get your audit.
  4. 4You decide what, if anything, you want to do next.

Getting in touch does not commit you to anything. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your details to anyone. See our privacy policy.

What we will eventually need

One to three months of statements covering every merchant ID you hold, in whatever format your provider issues. PDF or CSV is fine.

How we treat them

As confidential. We do not share them, we do not pass them to any acquirer or payment provider, and we do not use them as examples. We delete them once the audit is complete unless you ask us to hold them, and we delete them on request at any point.

Where we are

Ireland. We work with Irish businesses.
